Before you use this

This is not a legal clearance. No office in Ohio issues one.

This map doesn't know your tier, your conviction date, or when you moved in — and several restrictions shown here only apply to some people. On a few questions no court has ever ruled, and we use the stricter reading.

We deliberately err toward "restricted." Being wrongly told you're restricted costs you a housing option. Being wrongly told you're clear can cost you your home.

Full terms and methodology

Search stays on your phone. We do not get a copy.

Your search stays with you

People using this page can be in a vulnerable spot. A list of who searched which address could be demanded in court, stolen, or used to harass someone.

This site has no server that could keep that list. After the page loads, your phone only fetches map files from this same site. We do not send your address to a mapping company. We do not use cookies. We do not keep a search history. This browser can remember that you checked the one-time terms box. It does not store addresses.

What this checks

Ohio law bars some people from living within 1,000 feet of a school or licensed child care. A few cities add parks, pools, libraries, or rec centers.

We measure lot line to lot line, not door to door. Type an address in Hamilton County.

  • You, a family member, a landlord, or a caseworker can use this.
  • It is a map check, not a court order.
  • A lawyer or the sheriff can still reach a different result.
